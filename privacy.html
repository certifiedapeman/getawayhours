Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Getaway Hours Uploader is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the Getaway Hours YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope youtube.upload), the tool uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data.

What it stores

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or sent anywhere except to Google's YouTube API to upload the owner's videos.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads go through the YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply.
